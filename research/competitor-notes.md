# Category Review — Koreatown / LA matcha-and-coffee cafes
Limited review, for understanding user expectations only. **Nothing here is to be copied.**

## Who the category is
Motiq sits inside a dense, currently fashionable Koreatown matcha-and-coffee scene. Comparable nearby or similar businesses that surfaced: Rōk Matcha (Koreatown, `rokmatcha.com`), Matsu Matcha (547 S Western Ave, opened early 2026), DAMO (3510 W 8th St), Coffee MCO (Koreatown, multi-storey, Wi-Fi and on-site parking), and the broader LA matcha specialists such as Café Matcha (`cafe-matcha.com`, two locations, Shopify).

## Patterns observed
- **Most sites in this category are thin.** Where a site exists, it is typically a Shopify or Squarespace template carrying hours, an address, a menu image and a delivery link.
- **The common IA is short:** Home / Menu / Visit (or Locations) / Shop / Order Now. Café Matcha's navigation, for example, runs Shop All → Our Menu → Visit Our Cafés → Order Now.
- **"Order Now" almost always links out** to Postmates, Uber Eats or DoorDash rather than to first-party checkout.
- **Retail is the differentiator for the serious matcha brands** — those that sell tins and kits build a Shopify store around it. Motiq has no retail product, so that entire pattern does not apply.
- **Nobody in this local set is doing art-directed motion or scroll storytelling.** The category's web quality is markedly lower than the category's photography quality.

## What customers expect to find, fast
Hours. Address. Parking. What the signature drink is. A menu with prices. A way to order. Whether it is a place you can sit and work.

## Where Motiq can differentiate
1. **The photography is already better than the category's websites.** Nobody nearby is presenting drinks at this level online.
2. **Parking and hours are genuinely competitive facts in Koreatown** — free parking and 8am–10pm daily are real advantages, and most competitor sites bury this kind of detail.
3. **The food is a second business** the category's cafes do not have. A cafe site that takes a 16-dish kitchen seriously is unusual.
4. **The espresso programme is documented** — origin, roast, process, tasting notes — which is roastery-grade transparency in a matcha-led market.

## Clichés to avoid, seen across the category
Full-bleed generic latte-art stock; a centred logo over a blurred cafe photo; "Est. 20XX" badges; three equal cards reading Coffee / Tea / Food; an embedded raw Google Maps iframe with default styling; an Instagram feed widget dumped at the bottom of the page; endless centred sections with the same container width.
