## Global characteristics
- **Every single image is portrait.** Mostly 3:4, three are 4:5. There is **not one landscape frame in the set.** This is the defining technical constraint on any desktop hero.
- Resolution ranges from 1080×1440 to 3072×4096. All are large enough for web use; the three 3072×4096 files are large enough for full-bleed desktop.
- File sizes are small (156 KB – 716 KB), already reasonably compressed. Conversion to WebP/AVIF and responsive sizing is a Phase 1 task, not urgent.
- **Two distinct art directions, deliberately maintained by the brand:**
  - **Dark world** — espresso, hojicha, cream tops, food, interior. Near-black and warm brown backgrounds, single warm light source, deep shadow, amber highlights.
  - **Light world** — matcha. Cream, white and pale grey backgrounds, soft diffused light, one saturated green as the only strong colour.
  This is directly relevant to the client instruction to keep each section's background relevant to its picture. The assets already sort themselves into two background families.
- 6 of 16 carry the "motiq." script logo burned into a corner. 5 of 16 carry **baked-in body copy** that cannot be removed cleanly.

## Grading summary
- **A — hero / primary:** 16, 13, 08, 09, 14
- **B — strong supporting:** 03, 15, 01, 02, 10, 11
- **C — possible, with a caveat:** 04, 05, 06, 07, 12 — all carry baked-in text
- **D — avoid:** none. There is no weak file in this set. The brand shoots well.

## Duplicates and near-duplicates (documented, nothing deleted)
- **13, 14, 16** are three takes of the same matcha-pour idea. Different enough to use together as a sequence, too similar to scatter across unrelated sections.
- **01, 02** are the same table, light and camera position with two different drinks. Natural as a pair, weak apart.
- **06, 07** share one background treatment. They are a set of two by design.
- No byte-identical files, no resized re-exports, no screenshots of photographs, no low-resolution copies.

## Baked-in text — the practical constraint
Five images (04, 05, 06, 07, 12) have typography burned into the pixels. Consequences:
1. They cannot be cropped freely or used as full-bleed backgrounds without the text landing somewhere awkward.
2. Their text will not scale, reflow, translate, or be read by a screen reader. If the information matters, it must be re-set as real HTML with the image behind or beside it.
3. Their copy has been transcribed verbatim into `content/captured-copy.md` and is usable as brand language even where the image is not usable as a picture.

## Missing assets

### NEEDED
- **Any landscape/horizontal frame.** Every asset is portrait. A desktop hero currently means cropping a portrait hard, sitting it in a split composition, or building the hero around a portrait frame on purpose.
- **Exterior / storefront with the "motiq." sign.** The single most useful missing shot for a local business. Exists in customer photos on Yelp; we cannot use those.
- **The room, wide and in daylight.** The only interior we hold is a dark evening counter shot. Reviews and Yelp photos show a bright, high-ceilinged daytime room with a large covered patio — the thing customers actually praise most — and we have **no usable image of it at all.**
- **Logo as vector (SVG) or transparent PNG.** We only have the wordmark burned into photo corners and the round Instagram avatar.
- **Food photography without baked-in text.** One card is our entire food library, for a kitchen with 16 dishes.
- **Any video.** Zero video assets. The brand's own Instagram contains pour videos; none were exported into this folder.

### NICE TO HAVE
- The patio and the rooftop level.
- People in the room — customers working, a barista at the machine, faces lit properly.
- The pastry case and packaging.
- Cream-top pour in motion, which is the one motif we have only as stills.
- Vertical mobile-native footage.

## Third-party media
- Yelp holds 457 photos and 7 videos for this business (147 drink, 128 food, 59 inside, 39 outside, 35 menu). **Three of the videos were uploaded by the business itself.** Everything else belongs to reviewers. None of it is licensed to us and none of it should be downloaded.
- The brand's own Instagram holds more material than the 16 files exported here, including motion. That is the right place to source anything further, with the owner's permission.
