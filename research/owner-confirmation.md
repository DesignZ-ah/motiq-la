## 5. Questions raised by the video (added 2026-09-17)

### CUSTOMERS' FACES
The strongest single frame in the whole project — the wide daylight room at 00:12.8–00:16.1 — shows **dozens of identifiable customers**, several looking toward the camera. It is exactly the shot that proves the room, and it is also a room full of people who did not agree to appear on a business's website.

→ **Decide before it ships.** The options are: use it as-is with the owner's assurance, use a frame where faces are smaller or turned, blur or crop, or shoot a replacement at a quiet hour. **My recommendation: do not publish recognisable faces without the owner confirming they are comfortable with it.**

### THE BURNED-IN CAPTION
The video carries **"Weekend" at mid-left and "at Motiq" at mid-right** for its entire duration. It cannot be removed with the tools on this machine.
→ Confirm whether an un-captioned master exists. If it does, it is worth having — the caption sits exactly where a hero headline belongs.

### VIDEO RIGHTS AND MUSIC
Same question as the photography: this is an Instagram export from the brand's own account, obtained without direct owner contact.
→ Confirm authorisation, and confirm the audio track's licensing if the video is ever to play with sound. For background use it would be muted, which sidesteps the issue.

### HIGHER-RESOLUTION MASTER
The supplied file is 1276 × 718 at roughly 1 Mbps — an Instagram re-encode, not a master.
→ **Ask whether the original export exists.** A 1080p or 4K master would materially raise the ceiling on the hero. This is the single highest-value asset request in the project.
