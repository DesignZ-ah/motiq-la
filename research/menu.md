## Signature items — by evidence, not opinion
1. **The cream-top family.** Named a "Signature Drink" category by the business, six items built on it, and the name of one item is literally *Motiq Cream Top*.
2. **Misugaru Latte.** The most-photographed and most-reviewed single drink on Yelp (35 photos, 26 menu reviews), and the only item carrying a press claim.
3. **Matcha.** The business puts matcha second in its own three-word bio, ahead of brunch, and runs five ceremonial matcha items.
4. **Kimchi Carbonara Pasta.** The most enthusiastically reviewed food item.
